My Story Aug 20, 2026

From Accounting to Marketing: Finding My Way Through Data, Creativity, and Curiosity

How a switch from Accounting to Marketing led to Salesforce leadership, a consulting trip to Lisbon, a startup marketing role, and turning around two retail stores through data.

Hi! I'm a recent Marketing graduate from the University of Massachusetts Lowell with a passion for turning ideas into strategies and bringing those strategies to life through creative content.

Leading the Salesforce Leaders Group

During my time at UML, I served as President of the Salesforce Leaders Group, where our goal was to help students go from campus to cloud. Along the way, I had the opportunity to work as a project-based Salesforce intern with Hattrick Consultancy, which gave me hands-on experience beyond the classroom.

Consulting Abroad in Lisbon

My senior fall semester took me even further, literally! I worked on a global consulting project for Unicorn Factory Lisboa (UFL) in Portugal, where my team developed three international expansion strategies. We concluded the project by presenting our recommendations directly to UFL's C-suite team in Lisbon.

I learned something important along the way: opportunities don't stop when you're willing to keep learning and give your best.

Leading Marketing at FormMatch AI

During my Portugal trip, I received an opportunity from the DifferenceMaker team at FormMatch AI to lead marketing. By February 2026, FormMatch officially became an LLC. Being part of a growing startup gave me the chance to explore marketing from a completely different perspective. I experimented with AI tools like HeyGen, CapCut, Claude Code, and Higgsfield, while also gaining first-hand experience in market research and analysis, including TAM, SAM, PESTEL, SWOT, and competitor analysis. I also worked on developing pitch decks to support investor conversations.

How I Found Marketing

But marketing wasn't always the plan.

I actually started my education in Accounting, and after two years, I made an informed decision to switch paths. While studying accounting, I decided to explore marketing and joined Oizom Instruments Pvt. Ltd. with an accounting background and no prior marketing experience. That opportunity opened the door for me.

Then came my second marketing internship, where I got the chance to work with D2C brands on social media strategy, content creation, newsletters, and projects with international startups as part of a cross-functional team. Somewhere between strategy, creativity, collaboration, and understanding what makes customers pay attention, I fell in love with marketing.

So yes, switching majors was a pretty great decision.

Retail Leadership at MINISO

My leadership experience doesn't end with Salesforce or startups. I also gained hands-on leadership experience at MINISO, where I managed two store locations at different times, first in Nashua, NH, and later in Salem, NH.

At the Nashua location, I helped improve the store's sales target achievement from 73% to 90%. Later, after moving to the Salem location, I helped take performance from 90% to 131% of the sales target.

How did I do it?

The answer starts with something I genuinely enjoy: analyzing data.

I looked at past performance, identified trends, paid attention to customer behavior, worked on in-store marketing, and paired those insights with a strong focus on customer service. Seeing those decisions translate into measurable results was one of my favorite parts of the role. Along the way, I was also recognized as Leader of the Month.

What's Next

Today, I'm especially interested in the intersection of marketing strategy, creativity, data, AI, and entrepreneurship. I enjoy exploring the latest AI trends, experimenting with emerging tools, working in fast-paced startup environments, and constantly finding new ways to learn and grow.

Long term? I'd love to build a business of my own.

And a fun fact about me: I genuinely love analyzing data! There's something exciting about turning numbers into insights, insights into better decisions, and decisions into action. I especially enjoy looking back at the data afterward to understand what actually worked and what can be improved.

To me, that's what makes marketing so exciting. It's where creativity meets strategy, and data makes both better.

I'm excited to see where my career takes me, what I'll learn next, and most importantly, what I'll build along the way!
