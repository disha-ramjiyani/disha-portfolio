About

A little about me

I'm a recent Marketing graduate from the University of Massachusetts Lowell who loves bringing creativity, strategy, and data together. My journey has taken me from marketing internships and Salesforce projects to global consulting in Portugal, startup marketing, and retail leadership. Along the way, I've worked on content creation, market research, international expansion strategies, AI tools, CRM, and data-driven decision-making.

During my time at UML, I was inducted into Beta Gamma Sigma, an international business honor society, received the Robert J. Manning School of Business Departmental Award for Marketing, and completed the River Hawk Experience Distinction in Global Engagement. These experiences gave me opportunities to learn beyond the classroom, take on new challenges, and grow both personally and professionally.

I'm naturally curious and love figuring out "why something works, how it can work better, and what the data has to say about it." I'm especially excited about marketing strategy, AI, startups, and entrepreneurship, and someday, I hope to "build a business of my own."
