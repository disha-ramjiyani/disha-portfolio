Global Consulting Lisbon, Portugal · Nov 2025

International Expansion Strategy for Unicorn Factory Lisboa

A short-term global consulting engagement developing an international expansion strategy for Unicorn Factory Lisboa, a Portuguese startup accelerator, through a program run by GIOBA in partnership with UMass Lowell's Manning School of Business.

Note: the full project deliverable is confidential to the client. This page summarizes the publicly-shareable scope of the engagement, drawn from the program's official recommendation letter. The full report is available on request where appropriate.

The Engagement

Over an intensive on-site consulting program in Lisbon (November 22–30, 2025), our student team worked directly with Unicorn Factory Lisboa (UFL) to design a comprehensive international expansion strategy. The scope covered rigorous market and competitive analysis (including a structured SWOT), a global benchmark of up to nine relevant organizations scored across ten defined metrics, and an evaluation of market expansion models (franchising, direct investment, partnerships, and licensing).

Three Strategic Journeys

Rather than proposing a single expansion path, the team developed three complementary strategic journeys for UFL:

  • Luso-Brazilian Bridge: positioning Lisbon and UFL as the de-risked European gateway for Brazilian innovation, and the reciprocal bridge for European entities entering Brazil, leveraging cultural affinity and existing policy frameworks.
  • Accelerator Know-How Export: monetizing UFL's proven methodology in smaller, high-need European markets through low-resource arrangements like licensing or pay-for-performance.
  • Vertical Complementarity Partnerships: addressing capability gaps in high-value domains (Deep Tech, advanced B2B) by partnering with European players who bring specialized expertise or late-stage capital, while UFL contributes its Iberian pipeline, policy legitimacy, and talent.

Outcome

The client's leadership (COO Mafalda Freitas and Executive Director Gil Azevedo) recognized the three strategic journeys as highly actionable and grounded in operational realities, noting the work would serve as a foundation for UFL's international expansion decisions.
